## Previous Project Experience

### EAII Employee & Robotics Division Reporting Management System

A full-stack employee reporting and management platform developed for the Ethiopian Artificial Intelligence Institute (EAII), supporting administrative and operational workflows within the Robotics Division.

**Key Features**
- Employee profile and organizational management.
- Role-based access control and user permissions.
- Weekly employee planning and reporting.
- Task assignment and progress monitoring.
- Management dashboards and reporting workflows.
- Secure user authentication.
- Centralized management of robotics division activities.

**Technology Stack**
- Frontend: React, TypeScript
- Backend: Node.js, Express.js
- Database: SQLite
- UI: Tailwind CSS

**Project Focus:** Employee management, organizational reporting, workflow coordination, and robotics division administration.
